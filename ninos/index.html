Analisis de YouTube Ninos

Analisis de YouTube Ninos is a personal tool used by the owner of a children's YouTube channel to review the channel's own performance (views, watch time, retention, traffic sources, impressions and click-through rate) and to prepare improvements to video titles, descriptions and tags.

The application runs only on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public, it has no user accounts of its own, and it collects no information about the channel's viewers. Any change to a video or to the channel is applied only after the owner explicitly approves it.
