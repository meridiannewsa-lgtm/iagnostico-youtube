Diagnóstico YouTube

Diagnóstico YouTube is a personal tool used by the owner of the MERIDIAN NEWS YouTube channel to review the channel's own performance (views, retention, traffic sources, impressions and click-through rate) and to prepare suggestions for titles, descriptions and tags.

The application runs only on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts of its own. Any change to a video is applied only after the owner explicitly approves it.
